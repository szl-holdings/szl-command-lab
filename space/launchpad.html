Evidence boundary

A registry row is not a production certificate.

Listing and reachability prove only what they directly observe. They do not prove operational readiness, model quality, safety, authorization, regulatory approval, customer adoption, or investment performance. Each artifact owns its lineage, evaluation, limitations, and release evidence.
